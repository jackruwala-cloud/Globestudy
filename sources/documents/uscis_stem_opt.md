# STEM OPT Extension (24 Months) for F-1 Students (USCIS / SEVP)

## What the STEM OPT extension is
F-1 students who receive a qualifying degree in a science, technology, engineering, or mathematics (STEM) field on the DHS STEM Designated Degree Program List, and who are on post-completion OPT, may apply for a one-time 24-month extension of their OPT employment authorization. This is in addition to the standard 12 months of post-completion OPT.

## Core eligibility requirements
To qualify for the STEM OPT extension: (1) the student must be in a period of valid post-completion OPT; (2) the degree used must be a bachelor's, master's, or doctoral degree in a DHS-designated STEM field; (3) the employer must be enrolled in E-Verify and must be the one providing the training; and (4) the student and employer must complete and sign Form I-983, "Training Plan for STEM OPT Students," which describes the formal training program and how it relates to the student's degree.

## How to apply and timing
The student requests a STEM OPT recommendation from their DSO (who updates SEVIS and endorses the Form I-20) and files Form I-765 with USCIS for the new EAD. The application should be filed before the current OPT EAD expires; if filed on time, the student may generally continue working for up to 180 days while the extension application is pending.

## Unemployment limit during STEM OPT
During the STEM OPT extension, a student is allowed an additional 60 days of unemployment. Combined with the 90 days allowed during the initial post-completion OPT, this means a total of up to 150 days of unemployment across the entire OPT period. Exceeding this is a status violation.

## Ongoing reporting duties
STEM OPT carries extra obligations: periodic validation reports to the DSO, self-evaluations on the Form I-983, and prompt reporting of changes in employment or address. Because these requirements are strict and tied to your status, confirm each step with your DSO / international student office and do not rely on general summaries alone.
