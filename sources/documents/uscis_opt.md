# Optional Practical Training (OPT) for F-1 Students (USCIS / SEVP)

## What OPT is
Optional Practical Training (OPT) is temporary employment authorization directly related to an F-1 student's major area of study. Eligible students can apply to receive up to 12 months of OPT employment authorization per higher level of education (for example, one 12-month period after a bachelor's and another after a master's). OPT can be used before completing studies (pre-completion) or after (post-completion).

## Pre-completion vs. post-completion OPT
Pre-completion OPT: while school is in session, work is limited to 20 hours or less per week; during breaks the student may work full time. Post-completion OPT: after completing the course of study, the student may work part time (at least 20 hours per week) or full time. Any period of pre-completion OPT is deducted from the available post-completion OPT (pre-completion part-time OPT is deducted at half the rate).

## How to apply
The student requests a recommendation from their DSO, who endorses the Form I-20 and updates SEVIS. The student then files Form I-765, "Application for Employment Authorization," with USCIS to request an Employment Authorization Document (EAD). The student may not begin working until the EAD is approved and its validity period has begun. Applications must be filed within required timeframes tied to the program end date.

## The 90-day unemployment limit
A student on post-completion OPT may not accrue more than 90 days of unemployment during the total OPT period. Exceeding the unemployment limit is a violation of status. Employment must be directly related to the student's major area of study, and students are expected to be able to explain that relationship.

## Verify before you rely on this
OPT timing, application windows, and the unemployment clock interact with your specific SEVIS record and program end date. These are high-stakes work-authorization and status questions — confirm your dates and eligibility with your DSO / international student office before applying or accepting employment.
