# Curricular Practical Training (CPT) for F-1 Students (USCIS / SEVP)

## What CPT is
Curricular Practical Training (CPT) is temporary employment authorization for F-1 students that is an integral part of an established curriculum. It includes training experiences such as required internships, cooperative education, or practicums that are directly related to the student's major area of study and that the curriculum requires or offers academic credit for.

## Eligibility and authorization
To use CPT, a student generally must have been lawfully enrolled full time for at least one full academic year (with limited exceptions, such as graduate programs that require immediate practical training). CPT must be authorized by the student's Designated School Official (DSO), who updates the SEVIS record and issues a Form I-20 endorsed for CPT. The student must have this authorization BEFORE beginning work. CPT is authorized for a specific employer and specific dates.

## Full-time vs. part-time CPT
CPT is either part time (20 hours or less per week) or full time (more than 20 hours per week). An important rule: a student who uses 12 months or MORE of full-time CPT is no longer eligible for Optional Practical Training (OPT). Part-time CPT, and full-time CPT of less than 12 months, does not eliminate OPT eligibility.

## Key cautions
CPT is tied to your academic program and a specific employer authorization in SEVIS — you cannot work before the DSO authorizes it, and you cannot use it for general off-campus employment unrelated to your curriculum. Because CPT decisions can affect later OPT eligibility and your F-1 status, confirm the details with your DSO / international student office before accepting an offer.
