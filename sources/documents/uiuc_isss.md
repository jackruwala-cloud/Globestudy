# International Student Check-In at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign (ISSS)

## Check-in is required for new students
International Student Check-In is a federally mandated process for new international students at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign. Every new international student must report to International Student and Scholar Services (ISSS) and have their immigration documents reviewed. Complete check-in as soon as possible after you arrive in the United States.

## The two-step process
UIUC's check-in has two parts: (1) complete the online ISSS Check-In Form, and (2) report to the ISSS office with your original immigration documents during ISSS's designated check-in times. Bring your original documents and a separate set of copies.

## Documents to bring
Bring your passport, your F-1 or J-1 visa, your most recent Form I-20 (F-1) or DS-2019 (J-1), and your I-94 record. ISSS reviews these to confirm your record and complete your check-in.

## How this connects to federal rules
Completing UIUC's ISSS check-in is how your school completes your initial SEVIS registration, which activates your record for the term. It works alongside the federal requirement to have initial SEVIS registration completed within 30 days of your Form I-20 start date.

## Always confirm current details on the ISSS site
Exact check-in hours, deadlines, the online form, and required steps are set by ISSS and can change each term. Confirm the current instructions on the official ISSS check-in page before you go, and contact ISSS with questions specific to your own record.
