# Reporting to Your School & Initial SEVIS Registration After Arrival (DHS/SEVP)

## Report to your DSO when you arrive
After you enter the United States, you must report to your school's Designated School Official (DSO) and complete your school's check-in process. Your DSO then completes your "initial registration" in SEVIS, which activates your student record for the term. Registration is the action a DSO takes in SEVIS to confirm you are enrolled and engaged full time in your program. Until your record is registered/activated, you are not yet in valid, active F-1 student status for the term.

## Timing
Initial SEVIS registration must generally be completed no later than 30 days after the program start date listed on your Form I-20. Do not wait — most schools require you to check in on or before orientation, and some require it before you can register for classes or move into housing.

## What SEVIS needs before you can be registered
For a student in "Initial" status, SEVIS will not allow registration until the record shows (1) that the I-901 SEVIS fee has been paid and (2) a U.S. physical address for the student. So after you arrive you should have paid the I-901 SEVIS fee and be ready to give your DSO your U.S. address.

## Your school sets its own check-in procedure and forms
Beyond the federal SEVIS registration, each school has its own mandatory immigration check-in process for new international students — often an online or in-person check-in and one or more required forms, completed before or right after orientation. The exact forms, deadlines, and documents differ by school and are published on your international student office's official website. Always follow YOUR school's specific instructions; bring your passport, F-1 visa, Form I-20, and I-94 to check-in.

## Keep your information current
After you are registered, you must keep your record current: report a change of U.S. address to your DSO within 10 days, and report other changes as your DSO instructs. Failing to report or to complete initial registration can cause your SEVIS record to not activate and can lead to a loss of status.
