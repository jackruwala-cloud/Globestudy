# F-1 On-Campus Employment Rules (USCIS / SEVP)

## Hours limit while school is in session
An F-1 student in valid status may work on campus, but is limited to a maximum of 20 hours per week while school is in session. During official school breaks and the annual vacation, an F-1 student may work full time (more than 20 hours per week) on campus, provided the student intends to enroll for the next academic term.

## No separate work authorization document needed
On-campus employment is an incident of F-1 status. Unlike off-campus training (CPT/OPT), on-campus employment generally does not require a separate Employment Authorization Document (EAD) from USCIS. Students should still confirm eligibility and any limits with their Designated School Official (DSO) before starting work, and must maintain valid F-1 status.

## What counts as on-campus employment
On-campus employment includes work performed on the school's premises (including work with an on-location commercial firm that provides services for students, such as the campus bookstore or cafeteria) and, in some cases, work at an off-campus location that is educationally affiliated with the school. Employment that does not provide services to students, or that is with an outside contractor not providing services to students, generally does not qualify.

## Maintaining status
Working more than the permitted hours, or engaging in unauthorized off-campus work, is a violation of F-1 status and can have serious immigration consequences. Because work-hour limits and eligibility interact with your specific SEVIS record and program, always verify with your DSO / international student office before you begin or change on-campus work.
